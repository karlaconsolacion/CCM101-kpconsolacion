# Mission Reflection

Among the three major cloud platforms, **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** impressed me the most because of its wide range of services and its ability to support different types of workloads. Through this activity, I gained a better understanding of how cloud computing platforms provide resources that organizations can use without having to manage all the physical infrastructure themselves.

During the activity, I learned that AWS offers many services for **computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other cloud requirements**. I also learned that **AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** have many similarities. All three platforms provide essential cloud services such as computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and scalable infrastructure. However, each platform has its own strengths and features that make it more suitable for specific situations.

One of the most important things I learned is that **business requirements play an important role in choosing a cloud platform**. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit from **Microsoft Azure** because of its strong integration with Microsoft products. On the other hand, an AI research company may prefer **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** because of its strong AI and machine learning capabilities.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** also helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. A good recommendation should consider the client's **budget, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability, security, and business goals**. The best cloud platform is the one that matches the specific needs of the organization.

This mission also helped improve my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio**. I was able to organize my research, cloud platform comparisons, client recommendations, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and technical documentation. I also became more comfortable using **Markdown and GitHub** to organize and present technical information.

Overall, this activity improved my understanding of how cloud platforms are evaluated and selected based on **real-world business requirements**. It also strengthened my skills in researching cloud services, comparing different providers, analyzing client needs, and recommending appropriate cloud solutions. This experience gave me a better understanding of the role of a cloud professional and how cloud computing can support different organizations and their goals.
